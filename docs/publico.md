# Talk

Encrypted messaging between wallets.

**The source is private until launch. Once launched, it will be revealed: in full.**

## At launch

The complete repository, and the contracts published so anyone can verify them without trusting us.

## The policy

- **Privacy.** No accounts, no email, no telemetry. Encryption happens on your device and the keys never leave it.
- **Custody.** Your money never sits in our wallets. It's held by the contract and released by rule.
- **Respect.** No spam, no data to sell, no surprises in the terms. What's published is what runs.
- **Open.** Every line above is checkable in the code the day it opens.

## Until then

No token for sale, no contract address, no presale. Nobody here will ever ask for your seed
phrase — if someone does, it's a scam.
