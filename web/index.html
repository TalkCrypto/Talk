01Product

An inbox with a price,
and a reason to answer.

Most inboxes are free, which is why nobody reads them. Talk puts a price on reaching a stranger and makes the payment sit on hold until you reply. The people you talk to never pay again.

01

Open in one signature

No forms, no email, no password. Your wallet is the account, and activating it takes one signature plus a small transaction.

02

You set the price

Pick a number in $TALK. Strangers pay it; contacts you've replied to write for free, always.

03

Answer, and it settles

Reply and keep the payment minus 5%. Stay silent and after 72 hours 90% of it goes back. You never hold their money.

04

Sealed before it leaves

Messages are encrypted on your device. The chain stores bytes it cannot open — and neither can we.

02The conversation

The thread keeps
everything in one place.

Messages, timestamps and payment state live together. You never have to guess whether they replied or what's still owed — the thread tells you.

04Policy

What you get, in writing.

Our mission is one line: connect the holders of the whole crypto universe — and give communication its real value. Every chain, every holder reachable, every fee public. Fair launch on Pons: no presale, no team allocation, the creator buys at the same price as everyone.

Privacy by default

No sign-up, no email, no telemetry. Keys stay on your device and there is no data to sell — there's no account to attach it to.

Your money is never ours

Payments sit in the contract, not in a wallet of ours. It has no withdraw function, so the only funds that move are the ones the escrow allows.

No spam, by design

Reaching a stranger costs something, which is the whole filter. Once you've answered, they're a contact and the toll disappears.

Open at launch

The source is private until launch. Once it ships, the repository and the contracts are public and verifiable by anyone.

05FAQ

Questions people ask.

Do I need to deposit anything to open an inbox?

No deposit. Opening an inbox takes one signature and one small transaction (gas). You only pay real money when a stranger writes to you.

Who holds the money while it waits?

The contract. It has no withdraw function, so the only funds that can move are the ones the escrow rules allow.

What happens if I never answer?

After 72 hours the sender gets 90% back and Talk keeps 10% — that's the filter. Anyone can trigger that refund. You don't have to be online, and we can't block it.

Can you read my messages?

No. They're sealed on your device with a key derived from your signature. What reaches the chain is bytes nobody can open without your wallet.

What does Talk charge?

Only two things. When a stranger pays to reach you, that payment is yours if you reply within 72h — the protocol keeps 5% of it at settlement. And every trade of $TALK pays 3% (the split above). Opening an inbox, writing to contacts and refunded messages cost nothing.

Where do the trading fees go?

3% of every trade, fixed at launch: 1% keeps the communication infrastructure running, 1% funds development and tooling, and 1% goes to the multichain expansion — Talk will be able to speak across networks. The split is public and on-chain.

What's coming after launch?

After December 12: Talk across any chain — not just Robinhood. One inbox, every network. Mobile apps (Android APK + iOS) and custom avatars are coming too — the web app already installs on your phone today.
