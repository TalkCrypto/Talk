04 / FAQ

Questions.

Do I need to deposit anything to open an inbox?

No. Opening an inbox is a free signature — not a transaction. You only spend gas when you send or reply.

Who holds the money while it's waiting?

The contract, not us. It has no withdraw function, so the only funds that move are the ones the escrow dictates.

What happens if I never answer?

After 72 hours the sender gets it back. Anyone can trigger the refund — you don't have to be online and we can't block it.

Can you read my messages?

No. They're sealed on your device with a key derived from your signature. What reaches the chain is bytes nobody can open without your wallet.

Is the token live?

Not yet. There's no sale, no presale and no contract address published. When that changes it's announced here.
